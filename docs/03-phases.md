# Phases

Kanda ships in four gated phases; no phase starts until the previous exit gate is signed off. Each sub-tab is a self-contained build brief: goal, scope, workstreams, build order, exit gate and an ordered task pack for your coding agent.

| Phase | Goal | Indicative duration (solo founder plus contractors) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 Foundations | Legal path, partners and a working testnet vault | 6 to 10 weeks |
| P1 Corridor MVP | Kenya and Nigeria corridor live on Base with caps | 12 to 16 weeks, audits included |
| P2 Multi-corridor and FX hub | Five corridors, on-chain FX hub, multi-chain, off-chain reserves | 6 to 9 months |
| P3 Scale and federation | Consortium governance, PAPSS link, African gold custody | 12 months or more |

Durations are estimates to plan against, not commitments; audits and partner contracting usually set the real pace.
